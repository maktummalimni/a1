About Mackinaw Petal

A friendly, independent guide to the jackets you live in

We help people choose outerwear wisely, wear it well and keep it going for years — without hype, sponsored picks or pressure to buy.

Our story

Why a whole website about jackets?

A good jacket is one of the hardest-working things you own. It goes out in every kind of weather, carries your keys and phone, and is often the first thing people notice about you. Yet choosing one can be confusing: technical terms, sizing that changes from label to label, and care instructions that seem to contradict each other.

Mackinaw Petal began as a simple idea — collect the practical knowledge that makes outerwear easier, and explain it in plain English. Our guides cover the classic styles, the fabrics behind them, fit, layering and care, alongside small interactive tools that turn advice into something you can use right away.

The name pairs the rugged Mackinaw, a wool coat born of hard winters, with the petal — a reminder that clothing should also feel soft, personal and a pleasure to wear.

What we believe

Three promises behind every page

1

Independent

We don't sell clothing, earn affiliate commissions or accept paid placements. We never tell you which brand to buy — only what to look for.

2

Practical

Every guide answers a real question: will this keep me warm, how should it fit, how do I clean it? If a job needs a tailor or specialist cleaner, we say so.

3

Long-lasting

The most sustainable jacket is usually the one you already own. We focus on buying thoughtfully, repairing and caring well.

How we write

Researched, tested and written in our own words

Our guides draw on long-standing tailoring and textile knowledge, manufacturers' published care instructions and widely recognized care-labeling conventions. We explain them simply, check our tools with a range of real measurements, and review pages every season.

All writing on this site is original, and our photographs are licensed from Pexels. When we mention a fabric technology or style, we describe what it does — not who sells it.

Who Mackinaw Petal is for

Anyone buying their first proper winter coat. People who love a vintage wool jacket and want to keep it going. Commuters trying to stay dry without overheating on the train. Parents choosing outerwear for the whole family. And anyone who has stared at a care label and wondered what a square with a circle in it means. Everything on this site is free to read.
