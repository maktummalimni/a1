1. General information only

Everything on Mackinaw Petal is written to help you understand jackets and outerwear. It is general in nature and doesn't take into account the specific garment, fabric blend, finish or condition of what you own. Your garment's care label and the maker's instructions always come first.

2. Cleaning and care

Fabrics, dyes, coatings and trims react differently to water, heat and cleaning products. Before trying any cleaning or conditioning method, test it on a hidden area. For valuable, vintage, leather, suede, shearling or heavily soiled items, we recommend a professional cleaner. Mackinaw Petal is not responsible for shrinkage, color loss or other damage resulting from care methods described on this site.

3. Interactive tools

The layering planner offers general starting points; comfort depends on your body, activity level, humidity and wind. The chest calculator uses common rules of thumb for ease, and letter sizes differ widely between makers. The care-label decoder summarizes commonly used symbols, which can vary slightly by region. Please treat all results as guidance, not guarantees.

4. Weather and outdoor safety

Our layering advice is written for everyday use. It is not a guide to mountaineering, polar travel or other high-risk activities. In severe cold, storms or remote areas, follow official weather warnings and seek expert advice on specialist clothing and equipment.

5. Historical notes

Where we describe the origins of garments such as the Mackinaw, we present the accounts most commonly told. Clothing history is often based on oral tradition and limited records, and details can differ between sources.
