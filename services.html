Services

Personal outerwear advice, with nothing to sell

Our guides will always be free. When you'd like help with your own jackets, these independent sessions give you time with someone who can look at your wardrobe, your climate and your routine.

01 · For anyone choosing a new jacket

Outerwear wardrobe consultation

A relaxed video or in-person conversation about where you live, how you travel and what you already own. Together we work out which jacket — if any — would genuinely fill a gap, which features matter for you, and which you can skip.

  • We never sell clothing or earn commissions
  • A clear checklist of features, fabrics and fit points to look for
  • A written summary sent after the session
Enquire
02 · For confident sizing

Fit & measurement session

We show you how to measure yourself — and your favorite jacket — so you can compare any size chart accurately. We also explain which fit issues a tailor can fix easily and which can't be changed.

  • Chest, shoulder, sleeve and back-length measurements
  • Understanding room for layers
  • What to ask a local tailor
Enquire
03 · For owners, groups & clubs

Care & repair workshops

Small, hands-on sessions on keeping outerwear in great shape: brushing and spot-cleaning wool, washing down safely, refreshing water repellency, sewing on buttons and simple patches.

  • Online, or in person in the New York area
  • Demonstrations you can repeat at home
  • Printed care notes for every participant
Enquire
04 · For a calmer closet

Seasonal capsule planning

We help you map the jackets you own against the year's weather, spot duplicates and gaps, and plan how to layer what you have so it covers more days.

  • A season-by-season outerwear map
  • Layering combinations using your own pieces
  • Storage and rotation reminders
Enquire
05 · For libraries, schools & clubs

Talks on outerwear & textiles

Friendly, illustrated talks such as "The Story of the Mackinaw", "How Waterproof Fabrics Work" and "Clothes that Last".

  • 45–60 minutes with time for questions
  • Suitable for general audiences
  • Tailored to your group's interests
Enquire

Please note: we give advice only. We don't sell clothing, alter garments or accept jackets for cleaning or repair. For alterations and specialist cleaning, we recommend a local tailor or professional cleaner.
