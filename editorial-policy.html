Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Our purpose

Mackinaw Petal exists to explain jackets and outerwear clearly and honestly. This policy describes how we research, write and update our guides, so you can decide how much to rely on them.

2. Independence

We don't sell clothing, use affiliate links, accept free products in exchange for coverage or publish sponsored articles. No brand sees or approves our content before it's published. If that ever changes, we'll label it clearly on the page and update this policy.

3. How we research

Our guides draw on long-established tailoring and textile knowledge, manufacturers' published care instructions and widely used care-labeling conventions. We explain these in our own words and link to primary sources where they help.

4. Original writing and images

All text on this site is written for Mackinaw Petal. We don't copy articles from other publications or reproduce copyrighted diagrams, size charts or brand marketing. Photographs are licensed from Pexels, and the care-symbol drawings are our own simplified illustrations.

5. Practical, careful advice

When we describe something you can do at home, we explain the risks and suggest testing first. When a job belongs with a tailor or professional cleaner, we say so plainly.

6. Interactive tools

Each tool is built on commonly used rules of thumb and tested with a range of values before release. We explain each tool's limits on the page and in our Disclaimer.

7. Reviews and corrections

We review our guides at the start of every season and whenever common practice changes. If you find an error, email hello@mackinawpetal.com. We correct factual mistakes promptly and note any change that alters the meaning of our advice.

8. Use of software

We may use software to help check spelling, links and accessibility. A person reviews and approves every guide before it's published, and we take responsibility for everything on the site.
